3. People & photos

Add everyone who appears in your game (pets too!). If you chose cartoons, we draw friendly cartoon versions of them from these photos. If you chose your own photos only, photos here are optional.

Photo tips. 1–3 clear photos per person: face clearly visible, good lighting, front or slightly turned, no sunglasses or hats covering the face. Group photos are fine, but tell us who is who in the notes.
