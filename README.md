# Family Lore order form

The online order form for Family Lore personalised family games. Everything runs in your browser: your answers are saved only on your device until you press **Send my order**.

The page is a copy of `intake/FamilyLore_Order_Form.html` from the Family Lore toolkit.

`sample/` is the Williams sample game (`1047_Williams_Sample/08_delivery/1047_Williams_Online.html`), so customers can try the online game before ordering. It uses fictional sample art.
